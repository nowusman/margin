## Technology Strategy and Architecture (TSA)

Strategic framework aligning enterprise technology capabilities with business strategy.

## Enterprise Architecture (EA)

A discipline guiding enterprise change toward target business outcomes across Business, Data, Application, Security, and Infrastructure.

## Architecture

The structure of components, their relationships, and the principles guiding their design and evolution over time.

## Architecture Principles

Enduring rules and guidelines that guide how an organization fulfills its mission and makes architecture decisions.

## Architecture Repository

A system containing enterprise architecture data such as the metamodel, capabilities, landscapes, and related artifacts.

## Reference Architecture

A structured framework guiding consistent, scalable digital solutions using modular, extensible, and decoupled architecture.

## Architecture Assurance

The process of ensuring agreed architecture standards and principles are implemented and adhered to during realization.

## Business Capability

A business competence combining people, process, information, and technology to achieve a specific business goal.

## Value Stream

An end-to-end set of activities that creates a result for an external customer or internal end-user.

## Rationalization and Consolidation (RC)

A process to streamline the application portfolio by removing redundancy, overlap, and obsolescence to improve cost and efficiency.

## Application Portfolio

The complete set of software applications and digital tools currently used to support an organization's business operations.

## Architecture Blueprint

An actionable artifact defining As-Is, To-Be, transition architecture, and work packages needed to reach the target state.

## Architecture Component

A replaceable, nearly independent part of a system that fulfills a clear function and realizes defined interfaces.
